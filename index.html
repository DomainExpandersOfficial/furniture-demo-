COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I visit your karkhana to inspect the timber before paying an advance? +
Yes, absolutely! We actively encourage every customer to visit our workshop in Kirti Nagar Lakkar Mandi. You can inspect our raw seasoned timber logs, feel the density of CP Teak and Sheesham, examine the mortise-and-tenon joinery, and finalize your design over a cup of tea.
How do your payment stages work? +
We follow a transparent 40-40-20 stage payment process: 40% advance to procure and cut your selected seasoned wood planks, 40% after you inspect the unpolished raw frame (either in person or over live WhatsApp video call), and the final 20% balance only after doorstep delivery, assembly, and your 100% satisfaction.
Do you use any MDF, particle board, or paper foil? +
Strictly NO. We are traditional solid timber craftsmen. All structural load-bearing frames are built with 100% solid seasoned CP Teak (Sagwan) or pure Indian Sheesham. For wardrobe internal partitions or drawer bases, we only use branded ISI-marked BWP Marine Plywood. We never touch particle board or paper-foil MDF.
What if my apartment elevator or staircase is narrow? +
Rohit checks all doorway, elevator, and staircase turns during the initial home measurement visit. For large furniture like 8-seater dining tables, king-size headboards, or tall wardrobes, we engineer precision knockdown joinery that passes easily through narrow stairwells and is assembled inside your room.
What is covered under the 10-year warranty? +
We issue a physically stamped warranty certificate on our workshop letterhead. It covers structural joint integrity, warping, and termite/borer infestation. Because all our timber is kiln-dried below 12% moisture and chemically treated, we stand firmly behind our work.
